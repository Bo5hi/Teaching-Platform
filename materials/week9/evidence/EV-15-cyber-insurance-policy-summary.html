3. Conditions you must meet

From the policy wording, section 7 "Security and reporting conditions":

  1. Multi-factor authentication. The insured must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all remote access to its network, and for all access to email by employees and contractors.
  2. Notice of an incident. The insured must tell the insurer about any actual or suspected security incident as soon as possible, and no later than 72 hours after it is discovered. Call the breach hotline below.
  3. Use of panel firms. Lawyers, investigators, and other firms must be approved by the insurer before work starts. Costs the insurer did not approve may not be paid.
  4. Accurate application. The answers given on the application form must be true. If they are not, cover may be reduced or refused.
