Bluebird Web Studio Services Agreement (2021)

Vendor contract, provided by James Chen (IT Manager) on request.

Fictional scenario for training. All names, companies, and addresses are made up.

In short

Bluebird Web Studio built Northgate's website and online ordering portal, and still supports it. Bluebird's developers connect to the web server through the VPN. This is the contract between the two companies, copied below exactly as provided. Read what the contract requires from each side, and what it does not say.

Agreement text

1. Services

1.1 The Developer will design and build a new public website and an online ordering portal for the Client, as described in the Developer's proposal dated 15 February 2021.

1.2 The website and portal will run on the Client's own web server. The Client will give the Developer remote access to that server as needed to build, update, and support the website and portal.

1.3 After launch, the Developer will provide support and small changes of up to 10 hours per month. Work beyond 10 hours in a month will be quoted separately.

1.4 The Developer will respond to support requests within 2 business days.

2. Fees and payment

2.1 Build fee: $24,000, paid in three parts: $8,000 at signing, $8,000 at design approval, and $8,000 at launch.

2.2 Monthly support fee: $1,200 per month, starting the month after launch.

2.3 Extra work: $95 per hour, only with the Client's written approval.

2.4 Invoices are due within 30 days.

3. Term and termination

3.1 This agreement starts on the date it is signed and continues for 12 months after launch. It then renews automatically for 12 months at a time.

3.2 Either party may end this agreement with 30 days' written notice.

4. Ownership

4.1 When all build fees are paid, the Client owns the website and portal design and content.

5. General

5.1 This is the whole agreement between the parties. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.

5.2 This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Missouri.

Assessor's note: this is the complete agreement as provided (3 pages). James Chen confirmed there are no amendments, and that the proposal mentioned in 1.1 covers only the design and features of the website.
