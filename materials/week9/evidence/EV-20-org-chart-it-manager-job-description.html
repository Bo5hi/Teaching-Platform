Part 2. Job description: IT Manager

FieldValue
Job titleIT Manager
Current holderJames Chen (since April 2019)
Reports toChief Executive Officer
HoursFull time, Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:00. Some evening and weekend work for maintenance.
Last updatedMarch 2019 (HR)

Purpose of the job

To provide IT support and systems for all Northgate staff, so that the office, sales team, and warehouse can work without interruption.

Main duties

  1. Answer staff IT questions and fix problems with computers, printers, phones, and scanners.
  2. Set up new computers and user accounts, and remove accounts when asked by HR.
  3. Look after the servers, network, Wi-Fi, and firewall.
  4. Run nightly backups and make sure they complete.
  5. Manage email and office software accounts and licenses.
  6. Work with outside suppliers, including the website developer and the printer company.
  7. Recommend and buy IT equipment and software.
  8. Supervise the part-time IT support contractor.
  9. Other duties as requested by the CEO.

Authority

  • May approve IT purchases up to $1,000 each. Larger purchases need CEO approval.
  • IT spending comes from the "office and IT" line of the company budget, which is managed by the Finance Manager.

Requirements

  • At least 5 years of IT support or systems administration experience.
  • Experience with Windows computers, Linux servers, and small business networks.
  • Good communication skills and patience with non-technical staff.

What to do with this

  1. Record this as evidence item EV-20 next to every questionnaire answer it supports.
  2. Read the job description closely. Compare what it says James is responsible for, and what he can decide, with what Linda Harper (INT-01) and James Chen say about his role.
