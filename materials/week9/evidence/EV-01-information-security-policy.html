1. Purpose

This policy explains how Northgate Supply Co. protects its computers, data, and customer information. All employees must follow it.

2. Scope

This policy covers all employees, temporary staff, and anyone using Northgate computers, email, or the network.

3. Roles and responsibilities

  • CEO: approves this policy.
  • IT Manager: responsible for all information security, including computers, servers, the network, backups, and user accounts.
  • Managers: tell IT when someone joins, changes jobs, or leaves.
  • All employees: follow this policy and report problems to IT.

4. Acceptable use

Company computers and email are for business use. Limited personal use is allowed. Do not install software without IT approval. Do not share your password with anyone.

5. Passwords and access

  • Passwords must be at least 8 characters and must be changed every 90 days.
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) should be used where it is available.
  • Employees get access only to the shared folders they need for their job.
  • IT removes accounts within 5 working days after an employee leaves.

6. Remote access

Remote access to company systems must use the company VPN. No other remote access is allowed.

7. Backups

IT backs up all servers every night. IT tests a restore from backup every quarter (every 3 months).

8. Security awareness

All employees complete security awareness training once a year.

9. Reporting security problems

If you think something is wrong (a strange email, a lost laptop, a virus warning), tell the IT Manager right away by phone or email.

10. Vendors

Vendors who need access to Northgate systems must be approved by the IT Manager.

11. Information classification

All company information belongs to one of three levels. The person who creates a document decides its level and marks it on the document or in the file name.

LevelMeaningExamples
PublicCan be shared with anyone.Website content, product catalog, price lists sent to customers
InternalFor Northgate staff only.Procedures, internal memos, warehouse schedules
ConfidentialOnly for staff who need it for their job. Must be stored in a restricted folder.Personnel and salary records, customer and vendor bank details, financial reports, passwords

12. Exceptions and enforcement

Exceptions must be approved by the IT Manager. Breaking this policy may lead to disciplinary action.
