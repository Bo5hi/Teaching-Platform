ItemDiscussion and decisions
2. Security incident, April 2026

LH and JC reported on the incident of 15 April 2026. An attacker used an employee's stolen password to log in to the file server and copied finance files, including bank details of about 140 vendors. JC explained how the attacker got in and what was fixed the same day. The lessons-learned meeting was held on 28 April; JC is tracking the actions.

The board asked whether this could happen again. JC said the main gap is that many staff do not yet use multi-factor authentication (MFA), because the current email licenses do not include the needed features for all users.

RE asked about costs. Legal fees so far about $6,500. LH said the vendor letters will go out by 15 May on the lawyer's advice. DM asked whether insurance would cover the costs. LH to contact the insurance broker this week.

Decision: Approved $7,900 per year for upgraded email licenses for all 62 staff, so MFA can be required for everyone. Approved in principle to hire an outside firm to assess Northgate's security later in the year; LH to arrange.

Action: LH to update the board if there are further developments.
