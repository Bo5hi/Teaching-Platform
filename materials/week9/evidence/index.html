Northgate Supply Co. Evidence Library

Every document, export, and report Northgate can share during fieldwork.

Fictional scenario for training. All names, companies, and addresses are made up.

In short

These are the evidence items that Northgate staff can give you during fieldwork. Each item has an ID (EV-01, EV-02, and so on). Open an item only after someone gives you its ID in one of your interviews. Real clients do not hand you a folder of everything. You must ask the right questions to get the right proof.

Rules for using this library

  • Ask for evidence during your interviews. When someone shares a document, the simulator shows its ID and a link. Write that ID in your interview notes.
  • If James says "we don't have that" or "that's not written down", there is no file for it. Do not look for one. Write "no evidence provided". That is a finding too.
  • Write the ID next to every questionnaire answer it supports, for example "EV-04 shows 38 of 62 users enrolled in MFA".
  • Everything here is fictional practice data. Do not add real company data, real screenshots, or real passwords to your fieldwork package.

What to do with this

  1. For each interview answer, ask for proof. Record the EV ID you receive, or "none".
  2. In the questionnaire workbook, list the evidence IDs (EV, INT, SCAN) for every answer, and if you can, the row, line, or section that supports it.
  3. Deliverable: your evidence file should list each EV item you used and one sentence on what it proves.
