SCAN-03 Vulnerability Scan Summary: Northgate Supply Co.

One-page summary of the Nessus-style vulnerability scan run by the MCCoE assessment team on 9 September 2026.

Fictional scenario for training. All names, companies, and addresses are made up.

In short

The assessment team scanned Northgate's internal networks for known weaknesses. The scan found 18 findings: 2 Critical, 4 High, 7 Medium, and 5 Low. Most of the serious findings are on systems that are old, forgotten, or not managed by IT. Use this page as evidence. The full list is in SCAN-03-nessus-findings.csv.

The Medium and Low findings are listed in the CSV. They are mostly "hygiene" issues: old TLS versions, SMB signing not required, a self-signed and expired certificate, a default SNMP community name, unencrypted logins, an unapproved remote-support tool, and old SSH settings.

A simple way to think about it

Think of Northgate's network as a house. Each open port is a door or a window. A service is the person who answers when someone knocks. The Nmap scans (SCAN-01 and SCAN-02) walked around the house and counted the doors and windows. This vulnerability scan checked each one: Is the lock old? Is the key still the one that came from the factory? Is there a door that nobody on the family list knows about? From the street, the house looks well locked: only the front door (HTTPS) and the side gate with a guard (VPN) are open. Inside, some rooms have old locks. Your job is to also check the floor plan (the asset inventory, EV-03): is every room in the house on it?

Restricted distribution

Restricted. Real scan results are sensitive. They show where a company is weak. In a real engagement, this report goes only to the client's named contacts (here, Linda Harper and James Chen) and the assessment team, is stored encrypted, and is not emailed as a plain attachment or posted anywhere. Treat this training copy the same way in class: use it only inside your fieldwork package.

What to do with this

  1. Compare the hosts in SCAN-02 and this report with the asset inventory (EV-03). Write down any host that is scanned but not listed, and any listed host that looks out of date.
  2. Use the findings as evidence for questionnaire items on vulnerability management, patching, secure configuration, and asset inventory. Cite the ID, for example "SCAN-03, plugin 900237".
  3. Deliverable: in your findings, explain each Critical or High item in one plain sentence a CEO can understand, and give a fix.
